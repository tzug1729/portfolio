## Why I built it

We made this to exhibit at the 2025 Kosen Festival, my college's school festival.
That year, each group in my class decided on its own exhibit.

## What I did

I worked as the team's project manager and as one of the programmers on Hisayoshi.
As a programmer I was responsible for the multiplayer system, the chat feature, and improvements to the UI.

## Where I got stuck

This was my first team project and my first large one, so I did not know the basics of development. I ran into merge conflicts often, and I could not keep the whole team moving well.

## What I learned

I learned first-hand why splitting files matters, along with basic development practices such as team management and scheduling. This was also where I picked up practical use of GitHub.
