## Why I built it

I built this to show interviewers, recruiters, and other engineers what I have worked on so far.

## What I did

For the web framework I used Astro, which came into wide use around 2025.

I had originally built the site with a plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript setup, but I came across Astro in 2026 and switched over. I also moved the design away from a technical look, toward something that carries both Dragon Quest, where everything in my life started, and the sense of refinement I get from the orchestral music I listen to.
I used Claude Code for the migration to Astro.
At the same time I changed how the content is written, from putting everything into config.js to writing Markdown and building it.

## Where I got stuck

Before the Astro migration: I had trouble with the loop logic for the certifications carousel and with laying out content inside modals. When I added i18n, Japanese kept slipping through, and making the site fully English took real effort.

After the Astro migration: I had learned from all that, so I thought carefully about prompt engineering to avoid repeating it, and rebuilt the portfolio. Laying out the content was what troubled me most: how to arrange it so that it is easy for everyone to read, and so that the controls are easy to understand.

## What I learned

I learned the basic structure of HTML and CSS and how to write them. I also learned how important it is to build while thinking as one of the people who will actually look at the site.
